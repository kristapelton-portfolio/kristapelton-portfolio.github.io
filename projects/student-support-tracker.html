Student Support Team Tracker

An Excel case-management and reporting system connecting referrals, meetings, action items, family contacts, and case closure.

ExcelOperational reportingData design
Workbook-based case study

From referrals to measurable follow-through

I designed an Excel-based operational tracking system to organize the student support team workflow across referrals, meetings, action assignments, family contacts, and case closure. The workbook connects records using case identifiers and provides manager-focused views for prioritizing work.

Problem & design approach

Support teams need to coordinate multiple handoffs without losing track of ownership, deadlines, or outcomes. I structured separate input tables for each operational activity, centralized configurable dropdown values, and built reporting views around actionability rather than simply storing records.

Implemented components verified in the workbook

  • 16 worksheets spanning intake, workflow tracking, reference data, and reporting.
  • Five dashboards: overall case overview, meetings, action items, family contacts, and case closure reporting.
  • Five structured Excel tables and five charts to support consistent entry and summary reporting.
  • Formula-based lookups and summaries connecting case identifiers, student information, ownership, and follow-up activity.
  • Configurable settings for schools, grades, case managers, referral sources, priority levels, meeting types, actions, and closure outcomes.

Operational questions the tool helps answer

  • How many cases are active or closed, and how is workload distributed?
  • Which action items or family contacts require follow-up?
  • What are the most common referral reasons and sources?
  • Which meetings and case reviews are coming up?

Quality assurance & limitations

The supplied workbook contains example records and calculated dashboard fields. A cached #VALUE! result was observed in the Meetings Dashboard; that area needs formula validation before this workbook should be presented as production-ready. Dashboard counts depend on workbook data and calculation state and are not evidence of real-world intervention outcomes.

Technical implementation highlights

The design separates structured referral, meeting, action, and contact records from dashboard summaries. Configurable dropdowns standardize categorical inputs, while lookup and aggregation formulas connect case IDs, owners, deadlines, and status indicators. The overview visualizes workload distribution and exception queues, including overdue actions and family follow-ups.

Privacy and scope: The screenshot displays example aggregate dashboard information; no identifiable student records are published. This is an operational coordination tool, not a replacement for approved student information or confidential case documentation systems.
