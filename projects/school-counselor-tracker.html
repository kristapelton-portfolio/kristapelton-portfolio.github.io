School Counselor Use of Time Tracker

An ASCA-aligned Excel reporting template that brings together counseling service logs and duty schedules to help examine how counselor time is allocated.

ExcelTime allocationReporting automationDashboard design
Project case study · Workbook reviewed

From fragmented activity logs to an actionable time-use view

The challenge. Counseling work spans direct student services, family communication, school meetings, and assigned duties. Tracking these activities separately makes it difficult to explain where time goes and identify competing demands.

The solution. I developed a multi-sheet Excel workbook with dedicated entry areas and a consolidated calculation layer, designed to support consistent reporting of counseling activity across a school year.

Workbook architecture

  • Dashboard: front-end reporting view with visual summaries.
  • Individual, Groups, and Lessons: distinct logs for service delivery, with dates, grade levels, time spent, and intervention or lesson categories.
  • Parent Contact and Meetings: records for family communication and school-team activity.
  • Duties: a weekday schedule with calculated weekly duty minutes.
  • Info: configurable category lists for grade levels, intervention types, meeting types, contact methods, and time-use classifications.
  • Calculations: supporting formulas and monthly aggregation fields that feed the reporting workflow.

What the analysis supports

  • Comparing time spent across service-delivery and operational activity categories.
  • Reviewing activity and minutes by month and by grade where recorded.
  • Identifying how recurring duties affect available counseling time.
  • Communicating workload patterns to administrators using charts rather than disconnected logs.

Technical implementation

The supplied workbook contains nine worksheets, 12 Excel charts, four Excel table objects, and formula-driven calculation areas. Several log sheets include derived date fields, while the Duties sheet calculates scheduled minutes by weekday. The design separates data entry, configuration, calculation, and presentation—an approach also used in larger analytics systems.

Technical implementation highlights

Separate activity logs capture individual services, groups, lessons, parent contacts, meetings, and duties. The calculations worksheet aggregates time and category fields into dashboard measures, enabling comparisons of direct versus indirect service, intervention types, grade-level allocation, and activity mix. Chart percentages depend on the sample records and formula recalculation.

Scope and data quality: The workbook is labeled ASCA aligned, but this case study does not claim formal ASCA certification or validated compliance. Example entries are present; they are not represented as real counseling outcomes. Formulas and chart structures were inspected, but full recalculation and production testing were not performed.
